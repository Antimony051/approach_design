# Sidewalk measurement benchmarks for PaveWatch

November 17, 2026. Based on the supplied [Sidewalk Standards DOCX (SRC-047)](2026-11-17-sidewalk-standards/Sidewalk_Standards_Federal_and_Mississippi_2026.docx) and its [readable extract (SRC-048)](2026-11-17-sidewalk-standards/sidewalk-standards-text.txt). The benchmarks below help the team choose candidate test targets for local height, opening width, slope and coverage. The current project acceptance criteria are in [requirements](../../system/requirements.md).

## Dimensions to carry into target selection

Metric equivalents here are direct conversions from the cited inch dimensions. Keep the original units and ratios when reporting a benchmark because official publications round some metric equivalents (for example, 6.4 mm and 13 mm).

| Feature | Reference dimension or threshold | Measurement implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abrupt step | Up to **¼ in (6.35 mm)** can be vertical where ADA §303 or PROWAG R302.6.2 applies | Measure signed local height difference across the edge and retain the absolute step for screening. Measure both sides and record whether the edge is abrupt or beveled. [ADA §303](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch03/), [PROWAG R302.6.2](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| Bevel and larger transition | Over **¼ in** through **½ in (12.7 mm)** uses a bevel no steeper than **1:2**. Above ½ in, use the transition criteria for the governing source. ADA §303.4 references ramps or curb ramps. PROWAG R302.6.2 uses **1:12** maximum through 6 in and R407 for larger changes. | Measure the rise and horizontal profile. Record the feature type because curb ramps and blended transitions have specific surface rules under R304.5.4. [ADA §303](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch03/), [PROWAG R302.6.2 and R304.5.4](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| Joint, grate or other horizontal opening | The opening must prevent passage of a sphere **larger than ½ in (12.7 mm)** where the provision applies. Elongated openings also have orientation rules. PROWAG treats rail flangeways separately. | An image can screen for visible aperture and orientation. An independent physical gauge can verify clear passage. Keep crack detection and aperture screening as distinct measurement tasks. [ADA §302.3](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch03/), [PROWAG R302.6.3–.4](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| Walking surface slope | ADA §403.3 uses **5% running**, **1:48 cross slope (≈2.083%)**. PROWAG R302.4–.5 uses these as common references with roadway grade and crossing provisions. | Measure along and across the travel direction. Save the measurement baseline, roadway grade and crossing control where pertinent. [ADA §403.3](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch04/), [PROWAG R302.4–.5](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| Clear width | **36 in (914.4 mm)** ADA §403.5.1 reference, with a limited **32 in (812.8 mm)** reduction. **48 in (1,219.2 mm)** PROWAG R302.2 reference, excluding curb and subject to its exceptions. | Capture both boundaries and the obstruction footprint. Record the narrowest clear width and the length of a constriction. [ADA §403.5.1](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch04/), [PROWAG R302.2](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| Curb ramp and detectable warnings | Common PROWAG ramp references are **1:12** running slope, **1:48** cross slope and **48 in** clear width, subject to the ramp and crossing provisions. Warning strip depth is **≥24 in (609.6 mm)**. Domes are **0.2 in (5.08 mm)** high, **0.9–1.4 in (22.86–35.56 mm)** across the base and **1.6–2.4 in (40.64–60.96 mm)** center to center. | Treat ramp runs, landings and transitions as individual surfaces. Score visibility of the warning strip and dome pattern separately from measured dome height. [PROWAG R304–R305](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) |
| MDOT sidewalk design | Manual §14-2.06.05.3 gives **5 ft (1.524 m)** minimum sidewalk width, **4 ft (1.219 m)** of clear path where appurtenances are present, **5%** running grade with an existing-roadway provision and **2.0%** maximum cross slope. It gives **1.5%** as the recommended upper design value for construction tolerance and **1.0%** as the recommended drainage minimum. | Compare against the relevant MDOT project criteria. Give the **2.0%** measurement limit and the **1.5%** design value different labels when evaluating slope. Apply curb ramp drawings by project and revision. [MDOT 2020 Roadway Design Manual, pp. 14-16–14-18](https://mdot.ms.gov/documents/Roadway%20Design/Standards/Manuals/2020%20Roadway%20Design%20Manual.pdf) |

## Select a benchmark for the site

- **Building/site routes and public rights-of-way.** Record the facility type and source section. Use the ADA Standards for covered building/site routes and PROWAG as the right-of-way technical benchmark. DOT adopted PROWAG for scoped new or altered public-right-of-way transit stops after January 17, 2025, with a grandfather provision in §37.9(b)(2). The Access Board also lists a GSA adoption under the Federal Management Regulation. Check the adoption and site facts before assigning a binding status. [Access Board adoption history](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/), [49 CFR §37.9(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-A/part-37/subpart-A/section-37.9)
- **Mississippi scope.** The supplied guide summarizes Miss. Code §§21-37-3, -5 and -6 as municipal sidewalk authority, local specification authority and a curb ramp duty for specified crosswalk work. Its §§43-6-101, -103 and -107 discussion concerns specified publicly funded buildings and their entrance walks and ramps. It cites a 48 in entrance-walk width and a 5% grade reference with a handrail provision. The state section summaries are from the supplied guide's 2025 citations. Confirm the current code, local ordinance, federal scope and project specifications for a site review. The guide's §1 MDOT row points to M7, which is a state ramp statute. Its §4 and source register correctly give D1–D2 for MDOT. [Supplied guide §§3–4 and 7](2026-11-17-sidewalk-standards/sidewalk-standards-text.txt), [Mississippi Secretary of State code access](https://www.sos.ms.gov/publications-external-affairs/mississippi-law)
- **Inspection record.** Save location and route type, measured dimension and units, uncertainty, sampling and coverage, profile or fit baseline, photo/scan evidence, cited threshold and source revision, and any documented exception. Where geometry is missing or uncertainty spans a threshold, label the case for field verification. Record maintenance priority and any applicability determination as separate fields.

## Implications for resolution and accuracy targets

Use **ground sample spacing**, **local differential height error**, **width error**, and **slope error in percentage points** as separate design quantities. S2 currently evaluates 100–150 mm features, S3 allows up to 60 mm height error for its stated test, and S5 uses cracks at least 20 mm wide. Resolving a 6.35 mm step, a 5.08 mm dome height, or a 12.7 mm opening calls for separate bench evidence and later target selection. [Project requirements](../../system/requirements.md)

The estimates below use flat pavement, a nadir camera with 66° horizontal field of view and the same full horizontal view for both image widths, and a transverse LiDAR scan evaluated near nadir. Use `2h tan(33°) / columns` for average horizontal image scale and `h tan(angular increment)` for local LiDAR cross-track pitch. At **0.25–1 m/s** and a conditional **50 Hz**, along-track scan pitch is **5–20 mm** at all listed heights. The 50 Hz and 0.25°–0.33° combination needs confirmation on the sensor. These are sampling calculations from project planning inputs. [Acquisition plan](../../subsystems/03-sensor-payload-acquisition/README.md), [Timing and bench profile](../../integration/camera-lidar-timing.md)

| Pavement distance | Camera at 4608 columns, mm/px | Camera at 2304 columns, mm/px | LiDAR across track at 0.25°–0.33°, mm |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 m | 1.41 | 2.82 | 21.8–28.8 |
| 6.5 m | 1.83 | 3.66 | 28.4–37.4 |
| 8 m | 2.25 | 4.51 | 34.9–46.1 |

- **Openings and narrow features.** A trial heuristic of three samples across a 12.7 mm opening implies sample spacing of about **4.2 mm or finer** normal to the feature. The full-resolution camera's calculated scale gives roughly **5.6–9 pixels** across a perpendicular 12.7 mm image feature at 8–5 m. Verify edge visibility, contrast, blur, viewing angle and measurement error on known apertures. LiDAR's across-track pitch exceeds 12.7 mm even near nadir in these cases, so scan phase and feature orientation can cause a miss. Slower flight tightens along-track pitch only. A finer output grid adds interpolation and requires coverage and error checks against independent references.
- **Steps and tactile relief.** Test local height differences against measured step and dome references. Beam footprint, return quality, surface and scan angle, pose, calibration, and timing can affect measured height. For context, **3 ms** of residual timing corresponds to **0.75–3 mm** translation at the planned speed before angular effects. This is only one contributor to edge association. [Timing design](../../integration/camera-lidar-timing.md)
- **Slopes.** Measure rise over a stated horizontal run. On an exactly **48 in** baseline, **2.0%** is **24.38 mm** of rise and **1:48** is **25.40 mm**, a difference of about **1.02 mm**. A **0.1°** orientation bias aligned with the measured slope contributes about **0.175 percentage points** near level. Use both figures to judge whether the chosen reference, pose calibration and repeatability support the comparison. The error budget depends on the intended decision margin, the fit region and the actual surface.

## Candidate bench measurements

Use independent references for steps and apertures below, at and above **6.35 mm** and **12.7 mm** as appropriate. Include unbeveled and beveled edges, different joint orientations and contrasts, and a flat control. Use a measured incline and inclinations on either side of the benchmark for running slope and cross slope. Record baseline length, fit region, reference uncertainty, capture height/speed, valid-return density, coverage and reconstruction version. Repeat across scan phases and flight directions. Report missed features, signed bias, absolute error and repeatability separately by feature. Set the final target and near-threshold review margin after examining the measured error distributions. Track the resulting decisions under Q-12 and Q-13. [Open design questions](../../decisions/open-questions.md), [Verification plan](../../verification/plan.md)
