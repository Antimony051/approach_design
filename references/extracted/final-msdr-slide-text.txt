## Slide 18

DEMONSTRATION PLAN
Capst
one I 
demo
nstrat
ion 
delive
rables
2
Demonstration package: recorded survey, health evidence, processing results, and repeatable test procedures.
Abhinav Dhakal
Sensor Acquisition
PERFORMANCE CRITERIA
At least 10 minutes of synchronized LiDAR and image data, with gaps over 1 s flagged. Timing offset from the motion reference stays within 20 ms, and at least 90% of range errors are within 60 mm.
METHOD AND DELIVERED ARTIFACT
Bench recording of targets at 5, 6.5 and 8 m, with at least 10 observations each, plus independent timing events at the start, middle and end. Delivers the data package, timing plots and range-error table.
Shisir Baral
Diagnostics & Telemetry
PERFORMANCE CRITERIA
At least 90% of health updates (battery, sensor, storage, flight mode) reach the operator within 4 s over a 10-minute run. Sensor loss or missing pose data triggers a warning.
METHOD AND DELIVERED ARTIFACT
Timestamp at least 300 updates at the source and the display, inject five gaps per stream, and disconnect the sensor and pose inputs. Delivers the status view and the latency and fault logs.
Aalok Uprety
Data Processing
PERFORMANCE CRITERIA
At least 90% of reference points are within 1 m horizontally and 0.2 m vertically after transforming into a common frame. Detection is scored against S2, S3 and S5 (geometry, measurement and visible-defect targets), and three exports of 1 GB or more each finish within 30 minutes.
METHOD AND DELIVERED ARTIFACT
Reconstruct a known surface from three sensor poses and check it against at least 10 reference points. Test on geometry and image cases held out from tuning, and check each export for missing or corrupted files. Delivers the findings, evaluation tables and export logs.
