## Slide 5

FEATURES
Design features
PaveWatch  /  Aerovengers  /  October 23, 2026
05
F1 · Supervised survey
Define a paved area and flight route; supervise collection from the ground station.
F2 · Surface assessment
Identify broad raised or depressed areas, steps, gradients, and visible distress.
F3 · Evidence review
Inspect images, available measurements, units, and data-quality warnings for each finding.
F4 · Referenced data
Combine position, orientation, timestamps, and sensor mounts in a common survey frame.

## Slide 21

EVALUATION
Verification plan
PaveWatch  /  Aerovengers  /  October 23, 2026
21
Surface geometry
Use known 100–150 mm features, regions ≥0.5 × 0.5 m, and independent height/location references.
Timing and pose
Check events across the recording; verify common frames, mount transforms, and ≤5° attitude error in ≥90% of samples.
Detection performance
Reserve evaluation cases before tuning. Report counts, precision, recall, joint S3 passes, and failure cases.
Integrated operation
Measure three loaded flights, route tracking, operator takeover, power reserve, and end-to-end evidence integrity.
