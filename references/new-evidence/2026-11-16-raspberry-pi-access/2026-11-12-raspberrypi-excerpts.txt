Reinserting the card allowed inspection of the Pi's actual SSH startup journal,
which reported:

```text
/etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/090-picoscan-account.conf line 4: Directive 'ChallengeResponseAuthentication' is not allowed within a Match block
```

The OpenSSH 9.6 compatibility check had missed an OpenSSH 8.4 restriction:
`ChallengeResponseAuthentication` was global-only in 8.4. The supported
`KbdInteractiveAuthentication` directive is allowed inside a Match block.
This is confirmed in the
[OpenSSH 8.4 parser](https://github.com/openssh/openssh-portable/blob/V_8_4_P1/servconf.c).

`fix_ssh.py` replaced that directive in the managed snippet, verified the content,
and unmounted both partitions. Original snippet, startup diagnostics, and change
record are in `config/raspberry-pi/backups/20261112-010419-ssh-fix/`.
The initial setup script and later audit were corrected as well. No filesystem
repair commands were run. The next powered boot must confirm SSH login.
